# ABIIB

**Associação de Brasileiros nos Estados Unidos pela Transparência e Apoio às Comunidades no Brasil**
*Association of Brazilians in the United States for Transparency and Support of Communities in Brazil*

---

A bilingual (Portuguese / English) institutional website for a nonprofit association dedicated to
fostering responsible stewardship, financial accountability, and principled governance in support
of Brazilian communities. Guided by Catholic social teaching — subsidiarity, stewardship, and
solidarity.

## Structure

```
index.html          # Single-page site with all sections
css/style.css       # Institutional stylesheet (navy, charcoal, off-white, gold)
js/main.js          # Language toggle (PT ↔ EN) and navigation behaviour
```

## Design

- **Colours:** Navy `#1b2a4a`, Charcoal `#3a3a3a`, Off-white `#f4f2ed`, Gold `#a07830`
- **Headlines:** EB Garamond (serif) — gravitas, institutional tone
- **Body:** Source Sans 3 (sans-serif) — clarity and readability
- **Aesthetic:** Minimal, restrained, no marketing language; resembles a Catholic university
  or foundation page

## Tone Principles

- No language that accuses institutions of corruption
- No adversarial framing toward Catholic parishes or clergy
- Emphasis on strengthening institutions, not correcting them
- Language consistent with Catholic moral seriousness
- Prudence, respect for hierarchy, institutional maturity, financial discipline
